## 01. Real projection recovery

**Generic Sign Rigidity of Real Projection Kernels from Two-Point Data**

**Question.** Can idempotency recover signs omitted by the diagonal and entrywise magnitudes?

**Result.** For `N>=5` and `2<=r<=N-2`, almost every real rank-r orthogonal projection is determined up to diagonal-sign conjugation. A zero-subset-sum certificate and an explicit analytic nonvanishing witness establish genericity. The revised edition states uniqueness for the observed feasible holonomy fiber and explains why uniform injectivity is stronger.

**Scope.** Finite real symmetric projections on a generic full-support set. Sparse exceptional strata, complex phases, stability and efficient reconstruction require separate results.

**Evidence.** Analytic proof and an exact 1024-assignment check of the explanatory rank-one example; historical supplementary calculations are retained separately.
