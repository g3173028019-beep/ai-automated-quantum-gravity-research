- [30 · Nariai rigidity under null completeness](papers/30-nariai-null-completeness/README.md): This is universal-cover metric rigidity, not a classification of deck groups or of arbitrary double-null spacetimes. The proof takes Galloway–Ling’s null foliations as input and supplies the zero-twist, compact-warp and global-continuation steps.
- [31 · Classically matched vacuum waves with opposite quantum transfer](papers/31-graviton-metaplectic-transfer/README.md): Statements concern a marked frequency-gapped characteristic band with fixed gravitational normalization. The sign acts on the physical linear transfer, not on an arbitrary overall Fock phase. No global Peierls or interacting theorem is asserted.
- [29B+31+Q19 · Polarization defects, graviton transfer, and causal counts](papers/29b-31-polarization-defects-graviton-transfer/README.md): Exact vacuum sandwiches can have identical classical returns but distinct background polarization matrices and opposite graviton transfer signs. Marked causal-interval counts recover the signed transfer for arbitrary smooth vacuum sandwiches and recover high-frequency polarization from fixed-length chords. The probe claim is for the marked nonzero longitudinal-frequency graviton band. Polarization uses a marked plane-wave screen; periodic carriers may have infinitely many harmonics. The phase-free nonperiodic theorem assumes a continuum endpoint-count record or a finite design with a known resolving modulus. The result does not reconstruct arbitrary spacetime from an unmarked causal set.

- [32 · Type-II scale recovery](papers/32-typeii-scale-recovery/README.md): For at most r Type-II tree scales with known common Newton normalization, 2r−1 strictly spacelike band averages recover the distribution of inverse first massive threshold. The sharp deterministic Wasserstein error for r ≥ 2 is δ^(1/(2r+2)) without an upper mass bound; the full real-transfer record cannot improve the exponent.
