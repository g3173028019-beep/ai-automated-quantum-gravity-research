## Scope

The theorem assumes a fixed positive finite target, critical on-shell background, four spacetime dimensions and compact Cauchy surface. It is not an existence theorem for the preferred-clock effective action, nonlinear quantum gravity, a variable target or noncompact Cauchy geometry.
