# Absolute Interior-Spin Convergence on Lorentzian EPRL Edge Stars

The ordinary Lorentzian EPRL amplitude of the four-to-two move has an absolutely convergent full interior-spin sum at fixed exterior spins. The spin-cutoff tail is (O((1+L)^{-1})) in operator norm, uniformly in the real Immirzi parameter. The same local estimate extends to finite stars of one internal edge with connected closed simplicial links. It uses the exact noncompact vertex, normalized compact edge pairing, and one ordinary dimension weight (2s+1) per closed face.

For the four-to-two move, the full fine-side rank holds at generic dimension powers and at all sufficiently small nonzero perturbations of the spin-zero face weight. The **rank at the unchanged standard weights remains open**. At exterior spin (1/2), an even-permutation symmetry identifies one marked matrix element that is zero on the coarse side and would distinguish the marked maps if it were nonzero on the standard fine side. The paper also rules out a direct positive-measure representation of the individual all-spin compressed face, not positivity of the integrated kernel.
