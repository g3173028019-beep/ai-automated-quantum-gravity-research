# Finite Shared-Label Calibration of Lorentzian Volume

This analytical paper studies an exact sampling architecture: a shared classical label makes two null cut thresholds conditionally independent, while the native common-label tensor cut profile selects the volume being queried. In classical CGHS, a finite label budget is possible only for the local zero-mass linear-dilaton vacuum. In static warped products, finiteness on a compact regular tube is equivalent to an exponential-polynomial volume profile in the difference of the two Killing null coordinates. Full spherical tubes in four-dimensional flat spacetime require three labels; every regular exterior Schwarzschild null rectangle has unbounded exact label requirements under refinement.

The sampler's joint volume law is an input. The proofs concern exact finite mixtures of independent null-threshold laws, not arbitrary protocols for observing gravity, and do not establish a noise-resolvable rank jump or a quantum gravity theory.
