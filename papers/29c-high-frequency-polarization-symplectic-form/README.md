# Paper 29C. A polarization symplectic form at fixed two-stream null-dust limit

Two exact gravitational-wave families have the same scalar null-dust weak limit. The wavelength-rescaled Einstein symplectic form retains independent, explicitly weighted polarization pairings on their two streams.

**Scope.** Fixed marked characteristic sheets and same-edge cuts of the constructed double-null developments; a companion to 29B.
