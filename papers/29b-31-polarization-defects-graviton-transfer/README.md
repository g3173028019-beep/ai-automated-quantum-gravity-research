# 29B+31+Q19 · Polarization defects, graviton transfer, and causal counts

**Edition 2.** This manuscript supersedes the combined 29B+31 paper in place. Papers 29, 29B and 31 remain separate and unchanged.

Exact vacuum sandwiches can have identical classical returns but distinct background polarization matrices and opposite graviton transfer signs. Marked causal-interval counts recover the signed transfer for arbitrary smooth vacuum sandwiches and recover high-frequency polarization from fixed-length chords.

The original exact vacuum constructions, jointly retuned finite-wavelength matched examples, background Weyl readout, and physical two-helicity graviton bracket remain in the paper. The new sections prove a marked causal-count reconstruction of the full lifted transfer in a vacuum sandwich and of polarization by fixed-length chords. Transverse frames and longitudinal scales can self-calibrate for the sign; the polarization statement has its own screen and sampling assumptions.

The probe claim is for the marked nonzero longitudinal-frequency graviton band. Polarization uses a marked plane-wave screen; periodic carriers may have infinitely many harmonics. The phase-free nonperiodic theorem assumes a continuum endpoint-count record or a finite design with a known resolving modulus. The result does not reconstruct arbitrary spacetime from an unmarked causal set.
