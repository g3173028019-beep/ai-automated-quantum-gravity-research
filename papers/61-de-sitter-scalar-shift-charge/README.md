# Paper 61. A nonzero scalar shift charge in perturbative de Sitter gravity

A positive physical state with nonzero scalar shift charge survives all ten quadratic constraints.

**Scope.** Global dS₄ Einstein–scalar in the fixed eight-graviton TT sector, with a compact charge band away from zero.
