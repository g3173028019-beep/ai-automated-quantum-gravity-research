# Physical polarization of high-frequency vacuum gravity (29B)

**[Read the PDF](manuscript.pdf) · [TeX source](main.tex) · [Technical verification](VERIFICATION.md) · [Original Paper 29](../29-high-frequency-graviton-polarization/README.md) · [Full catalog](../../docs/PAPER-INDEX.md)**

What information about gravitational waves survives once their effective stress has been fixed? This companion reorganizes Paper 29 and adds an independent characteristic construction. It separates three questions:

1. **Transport.** Under the generalized-wave-coordinate hypotheses, the positive Hermitian physical screen covariance is transported along the null Hamilton flow. Its trace is the scalar Burnett defect.
2. **Preparation.** In the stated smooth double-null class, two exact incident vacuum preparations may vary their oriented polarization matrices independently at fixed resolved scalar dusts. Two harmonics per face suffice for the stated full-sphere factorizations. Both matrices propagate into a common overlap by screen parallel transport and inverse-area scaling.
3. **Curvature readout.** A calibrated electric Weyl test recovers the physical defect in the generalized-wave class. A separate optical bandpass recovers the prepared matrices from exact curvature in the characteristic class, in local (L^2) before the quadratic limit.

The one-stream rotating Rosen example checks circular sign and absolute normalization. The characteristic construction is not an application of the generalized-wave derivative bounds. It does not assert pointwise, unfiltered curvature recovery or a quantum-state result. Paper 29, including its source and PDF, is retained unchanged.
