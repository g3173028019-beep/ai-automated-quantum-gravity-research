# Verification record for 29B

[Paper README](README.md) · [PDF](manuscript.pdf) · [Source](main.tex) · [Original Paper 29](../29-high-frequency-graviton-polarization/README.md)

This record describes internal analytical and production checks. It is not external peer review or a claim of priority.

| Block | Checks and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Generalized-wave transport | Checked null support, wave-gauge quotient and trace convention against Huneau–Luk; verified the tensor connection coefficient by re-expansion with the standard Christoffel convention. Checked the anti-real Hermitian symbol test, weak-null transversality, and the skew/elliptic/null-form estimates in the three quasilinear frequency ranges. |
| Exact characteristic preparations | Checked determinant-one data, positive screen factorization, and the two-harmonic averaging factor against the vacuum Raychaudhuri equation. The main statement fixes the incident lapse to one as an input, distinguishes incident-source and transported-defect flux weights, and uses uniform characteristic existence and uniqueness after replacing the angular-hole-specific construction. |
| Interior transport | Checked the null lapse integrating factor, screen parallel transport and cut area, opposite-shear primitive estimate with the mixed norms, and signs and trace scaling on both optical families. |
| Curvature observables | Checked the exact rotating Rosen Ricci equation and positive-sheet helicity normalization. Separated the centered generalized-wave covariance from its factor of one quarter. For the characteristic theorem checked the calibrated lapse-weighted exact Weyl field, Riccati sign, one-variable filter estimate, and the local (L^2) limit. |
| Package | The standalone TeX was compiled with pdfLaTeX until cross-references stabilized; all final pages were rendered and inspected. This is a new companion directory. Existing Paper 29 files are not part of the update. |

Primary comparators: [Huneau–Luk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03470), [Luk–Rodnianski](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.08968), their [null structure equations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.1072), and [Touati](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17530v2). The proof is in the manuscript, not dependent on numerical replay.
