# Paper 74 — The canonical polarization phase space of gravitational and Yang–Mills wave packets

[Manuscript PDF](manuscript.pdf) · [LaTeX source](main.tex) · [Verification](VERIFICATION.md)

On fixed exact Einstein–Yang–Mills backgrounds, the action symplectic form on exact constrained linear wave packets, divided by the reduced wavelength, converges to the imaginary part of the joint graviton–color ray flux. This determines the projective polarization form at fixed intensity and the quadratic matrix Poisson bracket.

Two exact non-Abelian sandwiches provide a gravity-only test. They have identical local curvature data and the same aperture-integrated gravitational polarization matrices at every matched cut, yet opposite nonzero Poisson brackets between two gravitational polarimeters. A compact gravitational quadrature drive at the first flat cut distinguishes the late tidal responses. A separate phase-coherent two-cut comparison also distinguishes them.

This extends the project’s Papers 29B, 29C and 60 without replacing them. The results concern exact constrained **linear** solutions and a controlled optical limit, not a nonlinear Einstein–Yang–Mills weak limit, an unrestricted Peierls bracket, or a quantization. The manuscript was prepared with AI assistance and internally cross-checked; it has not been externally peer reviewed.
