# A signed Lorentzian Regge approximation theorem for the Einstein tensor

**[Read the PDF](manuscript.pdf) · [TeX source](main.tex) · [Technical verification](VERIFICATION.md) · [Full catalog](../../docs/PAPER-INDEX.md)**

Does the full tensor variation of a real Lorentzian Regge action converge to the weak Einstein tensor on an admissible refining mesh?

For the stated tangentially continuous approximation class, the signed bulk, face and joint terms give an order-two error in the dual of tensor H²₀. A nonflat vacuum plane wave realizes the assumptions on a mixed-sign family beyond conformal products.

**Scope.** Classical four-dimensional affine, quasi-uniform admissible meshes with uniform non-null margins and regular normal fans. It estimates variational consistency, not convergence of discrete solutions.
