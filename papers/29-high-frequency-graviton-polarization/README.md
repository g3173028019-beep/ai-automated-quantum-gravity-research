# Polarization transport in the high-frequency limit of the vacuum Einstein equations

**[Read the PDF](manuscript.pdf) · [TeX source](main.tex) · [Technical verification](VERIFICATION.md) · [Full catalog](../../docs/PAPER-INDEX.md)**

What gravitational-wave information survives a high-frequency limit after its effective stress has been specified?

Under Huneau–Luk generalized-wave-coordinate hypotheses, a two-polarization positive matrix measure is transported along the null flow. Its trace is the scalar Burnett measure. Exact vacuum sequences with the same limiting stress have opposite circular polarization.

**Scope.** Classical four-dimensional forward limit under the specified gauge and scale bounds. No unrestricted-gauge theorem, inverse limit, or quantum state construction is asserted.
