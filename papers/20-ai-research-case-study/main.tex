\begin{abstract}
We study two preserved artifact states of an AI-assisted foundational-physics
project: an initial scalar boundary-moment programme and a continuation that
inherited its manuscripts, calculations, errors and feedback. The later state
adds 580 relative paths. A saved hash comparison records 208 identical and eight
modified baseline paths, with none missing. Five baseline project manuscripts
total 85 pages. The continuation introduced positive noncommutative boundary
algebras, exact finite operational calibrations, source-specialized local gauge
benchmarks and explicit correction records. These are heterogeneous scientific
outputs and cannot be measured by file growth alone.
We reproduce the published filename classification on 315 new root Markdown
files: its selected fraction is 51.43\% of files and 56.35\% of lines. Removing
the ambiguous term MATRIX changes the file fraction to 49.84\% and the line
fraction to 54.84\%. Thus a majority-of-files interpretation is not robust to
one plausible rule change. We reconstruct representative mathematical and
encoding corrections, distinguish computation from analytic and physical
claims, and propose a compact evidence-routing protocol. The study is
descriptive: inherited context, unequal durations, changing tools and human
feedback prevent causal attribution to a model family or audit policy.
The released metadata and offline script reproduce the numerical summaries
without access to private conversations, model providers or the original
research directories.
\end{abstract}

\section{Research question and contribution}
Long theoretical projects produce more than equations. They produce conjectures,
code, source comparisons, rejected arguments, manuscripts and records of their
own status. Language-model agents can accelerate all of these activities.
Consequently, a growing archive can contain genuine local progress and growing
maintenance burden at the same time. The relevant empirical question is what
the retained artifacts support, rather than whether their volume sounds like
a successful research programme.

This paper asks three descriptive questions. How much of a later corpus is
inherited from an earlier state? Which concrete corrections changed the
scientific claim boundary? How sensitive is a simple quantitative description
of control-related prose to its classification rule? The unit of observation
is an artifact or claim, not a model invocation. We do not estimate the causal
effect of an audit intervention or rank foundation models.

The scientific setting is a boundary-completion approach to quantum gravity.
Its early primitive was a commuting family of thermal quantities $Z(\beta)$
and their positive moments. The continuation broadened this to noncommutative
boundary algebras, physical reductions, reflection structures and completely
positive operations. This background makes the distinction between a local
mathematical result and an integrated physical theory especially consequential.
It also provides unusually explicit examples of corrections: an incorrect
quotient type, an insufficient positivity test, an overinterpreted finite
model and a mismatch between a mathematical projection and its code encoder.

Our quantitative contribution is a reproducible reaggregation and sensitivity
calculation on preserved metadata. Our qualitative contribution is a claim-level
case reconstruction, with each example tied to an actual mathematical
distinction. The resulting protocol is a research-method proposal. It is not
an empirically validated optimization algorithm for science.

\section{Materials and chronology}

The initial state contains 216 files and the continuation state 796 at the
declared operational cutoff, 11 July 2026 at 20:37:59 Beijing time. The saved
manifest was generated on 12 July by selecting files using mutable filesystem
modification times. It is therefore a reconstructed historical dataset, not
a cryptographically frozen log created at the cutoff. Hashes identify the
included bytes but cannot establish when those bytes were first produced.
The present paper and its portability calculation were prepared on 29 October.

The continuation inherited the initial manuscripts, code, literature,
conjectures, failed routes and human criticism. It ran for a different duration
with different tools and task decomposition. These are major design

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Relative-path and content comparison}
Let $B$ and $C$ be the included relative-path sets for the initial and later
states, and let $h_B(p),h_C(p)$ be their content hashes. We count
\begin{align}
N_{=}&=\#\{p\in B\cap C:h_B(p)=h_C(p)\},\\
N_{\ne}&=\#\{p\in B\cap C:h_B(p)\ne h_C(p)\},\\
N_{-}&=\#(B\setminus C),\qquad N_{+}=\#(C\setminus B).
\end{align}
The saved comparison gives $(N_=,N_{\ne},N_-,N_+)=(208,8,0,580)$.
The eight changes comprise two root Markdown files and six source/PDF/build
artifacts of the projection-kernel paper. Thus the later state is largely
additive at the path level. This does not establish independent rediscovery,
semantic improvement of every unchanged file, or attribution of each addition.

\subsection{Filename-rule classification}
The published rule selects a new root Markdown file if its filename contains
at least one declared token, case-insensitively. The tokens are AUDIT,
CHECKPOINT, LEDGER, MATRIX, ROADMAP, READINESS, MAINLINE, CONSTITUTION,
SUMMARY, HANDOFF, WORK-SESSION, CONTINUATION-LOG, NIGHT-RUN, FILE-MAP,
DEPENDENCY-GRAPH, MATURITY, SANITY-CHECK, CRITERION, TARGET, OBLIGATION,
GATE, CANDIDATE, PROTOCOL, POLICY, PROGRAM and SCHEMA. Matches are substring
matches, not a semantic annotation of document contents.

For file $i$, let $\ell_i$ be its preserved line count and $s_i(T)$ the
indicator that its filename matches token set $T$. We report
\begin{equation}
f(T)=\frac{\sum_i s_i(T)}{315},\qquad
g(T)=\frac{\sum_i\ell_i s_i(T)}{77,375}.
\label{eq:fractions}
\end{equation}
The denominator is fixed across variants. These are deterministic population
summaries of the preserved table. Sampling confidence intervals would not
address their principal uncertainty, which is semantic validity of the rule.

We prespecify two transparent sensitivity variants for this reanalysis: remove
MATRIX alone; then also remove MAINLINE and CONSTITUTION. MATRIX can name an
actual matrix theorem as readily as a control matrix. The second variant
tests a broader group of content-bearing labels. Neither is offered as a
ground-truth classification. Removing ambiguous tokens can remove real control
documents as well as substantive mathematics.

\subsection{Claim-level reconstruction}
For each qualitative example we distinguish five things: the object actually
constructed, the assumptions, the evidence channel, the claimed extension and
the correction. Direct algebra can decide a sign or finite counterexample;
it cannot decide an unencoded analytic premise. A source theorem contributes
only under its hypotheses and convention map. An AI critic's agreement is a
record of further AI review, not human expert review.

The examples were chosen because the retained record contains concrete
artifacts and identifiable corrections, rather than sampled randomly from
all research tasks. This is purposive case selection. It supports explanation
of possible failure mechanisms, not an estimate of their frequency across AI
research projects.

\section{Quantitative results}

There are 335 new Markdown files containing 80,807 lines. Of these, 315 files
and 77,375 lines occur at root level. The remaining 20 new Markdown files
contain 3,432 lines. The Python source count increases by 37 while the
Wolfram-source count is unchanged. These numbers describe artifact allocation
and inherited structure. A source count is not a count of correct calculations:
one program may contain many identities, and multiple programs may test the

\section{Mathematical and encoding corrections}
\subsection{A commuting observable family is not a full boundary algebra}
An initial thermal family gives mixed moments
\begin{equation}
M_n(\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_n)
=\omega\bigl(Z(\beta_1)\cdots Z(\beta_n)\bigr).
\end{equation}
Even all such moments concern the commutative algebra generated by those
observables. They do not supply ordered insertions outside that algebra,
constraint reduction or sewing operations. The continuation's noncommutative
boundary formulation is therefore a substantive correction of the scope of
the primitive, not merely a new name.

A minimal example makes the limitation concrete. The qubit states
$\rho_\pm=|\pm\rangle\langle\pm|$ agree on every diagonal observable, but
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Tr}((\rho_+-\rho_-)X)=2,
\qquad \tfrac12\|\rho_+-\rho_-\|_1=1.
\end{equation}
Restricted ambiguity can disappear when the measurement algebra is enlarged.
To interpret it physically one must also show that both states satisfy the
same constraints, dynamics and limit conditions. The continuation consequently
rephrased completion ambiguity as noninjectivity of restriction on a declared
physical state set. This is a framework clarification, not a construction of
a gravitational landscape.

\subsection{Scalar sewing positivity is weaker than complete positivity}
For a positive map $\Phi:A\to B$ and positive scalar functional $\rho$,
\begin{equation}
\sum_{ij}\bar c_i c_j\rho(\Phi(a_i^*a_j))
=\rho\bigl(\Phi(x^*x)\bigr)\geq0,\qquad x=\sum_jc_ja_j.
\end{equation}
Thus every scalar sewing Gram is positive without assuming complete
positivity. Even all such scalar tests do not certify CP: transposition on
$M_2$ passes them but has a Choi matrix with spectrum $(-1,1,1,1)$.
The correct stronger test uses operator-valued matrices
$[\Phi(a_i^*a_j)]\geq0$ at every size, or an equivalent finite Choi/Kraus
certificate where applicable \cite{Stinespring1955,Choi1975}.
This correction is methodologically instructive because adding more tests
of the wrong scalar kind cannot close the missing ancillary condition.

The finite operational constructions in the corpus do use actual Kraus and
isometry arguments. Their CP claims survive this clarification. What changes
is the interpretation of a weaker diagnostic, not every positive result
associated with it. Earlier positive boundary quantum formalisms remain
relevant prior art \cite{Oeckl2013}.

\subsection{Two distinguishability questions}
A two-outcome finite instrument yields common-fiber posterior densities
$\rho_0,\rho_1$ with trace distance $|2\lambda-1|$ in its selected example.
If the classical outcome record is retained, the conditioned states live in
different summands of a direct-sum algebra and have distance 1. Forgetting
the record and identifying the quantum fibers is a separate channel.
The earlier correction changed a statement about the operational object,
not the numerical tolerance. This is a case where a correct decimal value
was attached to the wrong comparison space.

\subsection{Compression needs its declared algebra}
Compression $E(a)=V^*aV$ by an isometry is UCP. It need not be multiplicative.
For compression of $M_2$ onto its first coordinate,
\begin{equation}
E(e_{12})E(e_{21})=0,\qquad E(e_{12}e_{21})=E(e_{11})=1.
\end{equation}
The finite matched-charge construction is a star quotient because its
observable algebra is a block-diagonal direct sum and compression deletes
whole unmatched summands. Broadening that algebra to arbitrary cross-charge
operators invalidates the quotient conclusion. A theorem on one declared
algebra is not automatically a theorem on a larger, more physically suggestive
one.

\subsection{Code can verify the wrong projection}
An interaction analysis attempted to bound source degrees after removing a
nonminimal BRST quartet. The intended gauge-fixed minimal projection has
the conjugated form
\begin{equation}
\Pi_{\mathrm{gf}}=C_{\mathrm{gf}}\,\iota\pi\,C_{\mathrm{gf}}^{-1}.
\label{eq:projection}
\end{equation}
It is not generally naive deletion of nonminimal variables. An enumerator
using deletion rules can be correct for its encoded monomials while failing
to establish a degree bound under \eqref{eq:projection}. Repeating that
enumeration cannot repair the model-to-code mismatch. The corrected benchmark
therefore excludes the proposed source-filtration result and leaves the
actual anomaly-map image uncomputed.

The valid general quartet contraction is established BRST mathematics
\cite{BBH2000}. The useful project increment lies in its restricted,
support/normalization-aware specialization and explicit candidate primitives,
not an unsupported declaration that an interacting anomaly vanishes.

\subsection{A sourced benchmark does not derive its input dynamics}
The local gauge construction starts from an Einstein--scalar action and
derives compatible free Green matrices, normalization maps and BRST algebraic
conditions. These can be real technical achievements even though the target
action is supplied. They answer a compatibility question. They do not show
that a boundary-completion principle generated Einstein gravity.

The physical-state existence theorem also must be distinguished from the
surrounding functional algebra. A positive physical Hadamard state does not
by itself imply a normalized boundary instrument, descent of an ordinary
dagger to the same BRST complex, all-equicausal time ordering or an interacting
quantum master equation. The later benchmark preserves these separate limits.
This is an example of successful scope correction with a substantial retained
technical module.

\subsection{Decaying correlations do not prove information destruction}
One historical synthesis associated continuous spectral dephasing with
information loss. On $L^2([0,1],dx)$, however,
\begin{equation}
(U_tf)(x)=e^{-itx}f(x)
\end{equation}
is a strongly continuous unitary group, while the constant-vector correlation
is $(1-e^{-it})/(it)$ and tends to zero. A spectral recurrence distinction
is not a distinction between unitary and nonunitary dynamics. Neither this
counterexample nor a finite Page-shaped spectral-form-factor proxy decides
black-hole radiation entropy. The correction removes a physical inference
without invalidating the elementary spectral calculations.

\section{What can be concluded about the workflow?}
The record supports a mixed account. The continuation produced concrete
scope corrections, exact finite calibrations and local sourced benchmarks.
It also produced many mirrored status and obligation documents. The latter
can increase synchronization burden: if every claim is copied into every
manual mirror, a simple bookkeeping model has cost proportional to
$|\mathcal C||\mathcal M|$. This is a conditional cost model, not a fitted
law or measured runtime effect.

We use \emph{audit inflation} as an interpretive hypothesis: control artifacts
may grow without a comparable increase in independently closed claims.
The case provides examples of duplication and repeated deferral. It does
not provide a validated measure of closed scientific content at each time,
nor the counterfactual output under a different audit policy. Therefore it
does not establish that auditing reduced productivity. Audits also detected
real errors; the same examples can support beneficial error correction and
costly maintenance simultaneously.

An agent dialogue can reveal a sign error or hidden assumption, but role
separation is not evidential independence. Models may share project context
and the same unexamined premise. A fresh implementation, primary-source
passage, proof-assistant kernel or external observation changes the evidence
channel. These channels have different scopes: formal verification certifies
the formal statement, not its physical interpretation.

The comparison is likewise insufficient for model-capability ranking. Later
artifacts benefited from inherited work and corrective feedback; earlier
artifacts were produced under different circumstances. Statements that one
stage is better in a specified artifact property can be supported. A generic
claim that its model family is better at foundational physics cannot.

\section{Relation to other AI research systems}
Program-search systems such as FunSearch combine generated programs with an
executable evaluator \cite{FunSearch2024}; theorem-proving systems can obtain
kernel-level feedback \cite{Polu2021}. End-to-end research systems automate
larger portions of ideation, experimentation and writing
\cite{AIScientist2026}, while Co-Scientist studies a hypothesis-generation
and refinement workflow \cite{CoScientist2026}. These are useful contrasts in
the available feedback channels. Their reported results do not estimate the
effect of the workflow studied here.

For the historical literature context we pin the December 2025 first version
of PhysMaster \cite{PhysMasterV1}. Its later August version changes the
author list and evaluation description. Substituting current metadata into
a July-cutoff narrative would conflate versions. The June 2026 physical-science
multi-agent benchmark also predates the cutoff \cite{PhysicsAgents2026}.
This is an illustrative primary-source comparison, not a systematic review,
performance replication or common-benchmark ranking.

\section{A compact evidence-routing protocol}
The cases motivate a small release structure. First state the central
scientific question and the strongest claim that answers it. Give each
claim a type---definition, standard input, derived theorem, finite computation,
conditional application, conjecture or refuted implication---and record its
exact object and assumptions. A finite calibration and a physical continuum
construction should not share an unqualified verification label.

Second, identify the encoding bridge. For a program, state which mathematical
objects become arrays or monomials and why the encoded operations represent
the intended map. For a source theorem, state the hypotheses and convention
transport. A correct program or citation cannot compensate for a missing
bridge. The scalar-versus-amplified positivity example shows why test strength
must be specified, not merely the number of successful tests.

Third, preserve one current machine-readable claim table and versioned
history, rather than manually copying status across many active mirrors.
One short release report should state the strongest retained result, the
strongest withdrawn claim, evidence actually rerun and material gaps. This
proposal follows the observed failure modes; its productivity benefit has
not been measured in this case.

Finally, choose the next research operation by its scientific consequence.
A bounded proof or counterexample can resolve a question. Another criterion
document is useful only if it changes an acceptance decision or executable
test. A failed parent programme can still leave an independent framework,
method, benchmark or mathematical result. Requiring complete quantum gravity
before recognizing those outputs would discard the very distinctions this
case makes observable.

\section{Limitations and conclusion}
The study has one project, two reconstructed artifact states, purposively
selected qualitative cases and incomplete production provenance. File
timestamps are mutable, filename categories are semantic proxies, and the
technical modules have received AI review rather than human expert review.
Some analytic proof chains remain source-specialized and have not received
a fresh complete independent audit in this paper. No intervention effect,
general model ranking, waste percentage or comprehensive originality
certificate follows.

Within those limits, the numerical findings are exact properties of released
metadata: substantial inheritance, 580 additional paths, five baseline
project manuscripts totaling 85 pages, and a majority-of-files interpretation
that changes under removal of MATRIX. The mathematical cases identify
concrete ways fluent research can outgrow its evidence and ways correction
can preserve useful results. The resulting lesson is to align a claim with
its actual object, evidence channel and promotion boundary. Volume and
polish do not perform that alignment automatically.

The operator reports that earlier work was led mainly by Fable 5, whereas
results from July 2026 onward were predominantly produced by GPT models.
DeepSeek and multiple GPT models also participated in review, checking and
manuscript preparation and organization. This is a reported chronological
history, not a complete audited allocation of individual contributions.
An earlier result is not reattributed merely because it was revised or
packaged later. Human author names and affiliations are intentionally blank.
The results have not undergone human expert review. Automated checks and AI
reviews are further AI review, not human peer review. Historical source dates,
this preparation date and demonstrable public disclosure dates are distinct.
No authorship, ownership, publication-priority or journal-acceptance decision
is made. The operator states:

\end{quote}
