# Verification

This is an analytic manuscript. No numerical asymptotic fit is used as evidence.

| Result | Proof checks |
|---|---|
| Exact flat scheme | Analyze the actual marked peripheral relations. Nonparallelity supplies a unit in each edge commutator. The remaining ordered cap equation reduces modulo those edge quadratics and is symmetrized by an analytic coordinate change. |
| Actual action | Analytic Lyapunov–Schmidt reduction on the coexact slice, Bianchi to recover exact flatness, off-flat analytic holonomy, Artin deformation equivalence, and ideal contraction back to the convergent ring all enter before comparing leading gradients. |
| Exact cubic and quartic | Monomial support isolates the homogeneous leading potential. Row-wise coefficients are fixed by connectivity. The real analytic Moser argument removes all higher terms even at nonisolated singularities. |
| Unmatched spines | The split Hessian has rank twice the matching size. For the connected-ray-block class, compare ray and center degrees modulo the independent kernel quadrics. Blockwise Euler equations synchronize the normalization across every nonzero center edge. The off-shell equivalence is not asserted for arbitrary kernel support. |
| Leading coefficient | Fourier-transform each local pairing. The dyadic bound removes tails and tie strips in absolute value, and integration by parts removes the remaining rapid-phase boxes. An absolutely convergent tensor expansion extends the limit to arbitrary smooth reference amplitudes. The split Gaussian coefficient is taken only after this bound. |
| Closed comparison | Use the exact Paper 48 face theorem, not a semiclassical assumption. Its cover and matching leave precisely the singleton sites free. The oriented seam anomaly is zero by the Paper 49 formula and bipartite spectral symmetry. The quantum bounded-charge term and the classical coarea measure are kept separate. |
