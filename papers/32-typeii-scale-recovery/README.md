# 32. Recovering Type-II string scales from nonforward five-dimensional graviton scattering

[PDF](manuscript.pdf) · [LaTeX source](main.tex) · [Verification](VERIFICATION.md) · [Paper index](../../docs/PAPER-INDEX.md)

For at most r Type-II tree scales with known common Newton normalization, 2r−1 strictly spacelike band averages recover the distribution of inverse first massive threshold. The sharp deterministic Wasserstein error for r ≥ 2 is δ^(1/(2r+2)) without an upper mass bound; the full real-transfer record cannot improve the exponent.

Two spacelike transfer bands reveal even inverse-scale powers lost by the forward limit. The paper proves the optimal measurement count for continuous scalar summaries, compares the sharp forward and compact-mass rates, and gives exact two- and three-scale examples.

The setting is a positive linear mixture of projected Type-II sphere-tree amplitudes; weights share the known Newton coefficient. Recovery is in inverse squared mass, not relative mass error or the full microscopic spectrum.
