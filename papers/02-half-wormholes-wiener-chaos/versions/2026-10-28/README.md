# Half-Wormholes as Wiener Chaos

**Correlated-Replica Spectroscopy, Exact Pfaffian Sectors, and Boundary Moment Indeterminacy**

[English manuscript](manuscript.pdf)

The paper studies the one-time-point, zero-dimensional SYK polynomial with independent Gaussian couplings. Wiener-chaos grading organizes correlated-replica identities, a closed q=2 specialization of established sector variances, determinant formulas, finite single-draw bounds, and a moment-determinacy dichotomy at degree two versus three.

The exact half-wormhole expansion and general sector-variance formula are credited to prior work, notably Mukhametzhanov. The q=2 profile is a specialization with an independent derivation, not an original general variance formula. Ornstein-Uhlenbeck and hypercontractive identities are established tools.

The scope is this finite Gaussian-coupling model. A Lorentzian bulk, thermal/infinite-chaos extension and complete quantum gravity remain outside the result. Originality of the additional consequences remains subject to current literature review.
