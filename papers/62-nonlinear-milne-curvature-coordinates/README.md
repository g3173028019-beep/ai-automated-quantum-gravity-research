# Paper 62. Nonlinear vacuum curvature coordinates at a compact Milne cut

Projected electric and magnetic Weyl tensors and measured affine Cartan periods give exact nonlinear CMC vacuum coordinates. The zero projected-curvature fiber is the attained flat family.

**Scope.** A marked neighborhood of a compact hyperbolic Milne CMC slice in four-dimensional vacuum gravity.
