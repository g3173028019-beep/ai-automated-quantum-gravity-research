# A lapse-preserving ADM reduction of parity-odd Weyl-cubic gravity

**[Read the PDF](manuscript.pdf) · [TeX source](main.tex) · [Technical verification](VERIFICATION.md) · [Full catalog](../../docs/PAPER-INDEX.md)**

Can a parity-odd curvature correction be reduced to ordinary ADM phase space while retaining the geometric lapse, shift, and hypersurface-deformation algebra?

A local first-order reduction retains the original lapse and contravariant shift. The strong normal-normal bracket uses the reconstructed physical spatial metric; the paper gives separate covariance and parity-sensitive physical checks.

**Scope.** Four-dimensional vacuum on the analytic perturbative branch, to first order in the Weyl-cubic coupling. No higher-order or quantum constraint algebra is asserted.
