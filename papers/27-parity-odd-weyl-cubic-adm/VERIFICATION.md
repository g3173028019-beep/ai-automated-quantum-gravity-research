# Verification: Parity-odd Weyl-cubic ADM gravity

## Analytic review

The argument and its assumptions were read by a separate internal reviewer. The final text differentiates the new construction from existing results.

- Check the normal-acceleration elimination and boundary term, the unrestricted bracket using evolution but not multiplier equations, and the reconstructed metric map. The radiative and field-redefinition-invariant vertex tests have different roles.
- Glavan, Mukohyama and Zlosnik's parity-even reduction and earlier chiral amplitudes are prior; this manuscript addresses the parity-odd canonical realization and strong bracket.
- Four-dimensional vacuum on the analytic perturbative branch, to first order in the Weyl-cubic coupling. No higher-order or quantum constraint algebra is asserted.
