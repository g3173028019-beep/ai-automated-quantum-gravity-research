# Verification: Paper 48

[Paper overview](README.md) · [Manuscript](manuscript.pdf) · [TeX entry point](main.tex)

The internal review checked the Kirby and Hopf-tree presentation, the framing and the two invariant normalizations, both shifted-level parities, the corona remainder, the all-length path elimination, and the forced/free decomposition for paired trees. The signed possible-zero case for unrestricted graphs is not a theorem of this paper.

The included replay programs independently check the exact H contraction, paired-cap path elimination (using the literal opposite caps), corona factorizations, finite tree spin-multiplicity transfer and the odd-level SO(3) relation. The README lists the commands. Finite replays are checks of the exact formulae and do not prove the analytic asymptotics.

The TeX sources were rebuilt and the 14-page PDF was rendered and inspected. This is an internal analytical check, not external peer review.
