\begin{abstract}
We compute leading asymptotics of the uncolored \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) Witten--Reshetikhin--Turaev invariant for fixed tree plumbings with two opposite order-two caps at every backbone vertex. An exact cotangent sum gives an \(r^2\log r\) leading term for a six-component H plumbing in sphere normalization, where \(r\) is the shifted level. A family of irreducible corona plumbings realizes arbitrarily large leading logarithmic degrees; fixed integer framings give a polytope cutoff with a one-logarithm error estimate. Exact finite elimination on paths gives a log at each free ordered charge on even paths and a summable bounded-charge chain on odd paths. For general unframed paired-cap trees, the leading exponent is given by the maximum matching number and the log degree is the dimension of the maximizing fractional independent-set face. The coefficient in this case is positive after a known parity sign. These results exclude the literal finite-phase log-free Laurent--Puiseux ansatz for the H example and give no uniform bound on leading log degree across the corona family.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The accompanying scripts replay the finite Kirby and modular sums on small levels, test both level parities and all path lengths from one to ten, and compare the integer-spin factor. They are checks on the normalizations; the proofs do not rely on floating-point evaluation.
